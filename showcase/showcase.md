# Fathom

A small light hangs in an ocean of black. You are a glowing forager, swimming
the flooded passages of a buried maze. Eat the scattered plankton, find the way
around the rock, and leave no mote behind. Clear the maze and descend into water
with more hunters—and less reach for your sonar.

Your glow uncovers the passages nearby. Sonar runs around corners, sketching
routes into memory and catching brief glimpses of what waits ahead. In the
Kindle dive, even remembered ground disappears beyond your circular window.
Eating widens that window, but every bright mouthful makes you easier to find.

An amber glimmer could be a harmless drifter worth chasing. It could also be a
Lanternjaw wearing a jellyfish's shape. The eyeless Gloamfin listens for your
sonar and answers with violet waves of its own. Somewhere beyond your light,
a Flarefish charges a bloom that burns straight through the rock.

Choose when to shine and when to listen. Throw ink across a hunter's sightline,
turn a corner before the Gloamfin catches up, and slip through the wrap tunnel
to emerge on the other side. Three reserve lives give you room to learn the
dark. The next depth asks you to know it.
